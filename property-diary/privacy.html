Privacy Policy: Property Diary

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Aurezian Technologies

Property Diary is an app for keeping your own list of properties for sale or rent (houses, plots, shops, files and more), with photos and private owner notes. This policy explains what happens to the information you put in the app.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not have servers for this app, we do not collect your data, and we cannot see your listings, photos or owner details.

Information you enter

Listings, photos, owner names, phone numbers, prices, commission and notes are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos

When you press "Add photos", Android lets you choose pictures. The app makes a smaller copy of each picture you pick and keeps it inside the app. It does not read any other photos on your phone.

Sharing

When you press "Share with photos", "WhatsApp" or "Copy ad text", the app hands the listing text (and photos, if you chose that) to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp. Owner details marked "Private" are never included in shared text. What happens after that is covered by the privacy policy of the app you shared to.

Backups

If you press "Save backup file", the app creates a file with your listings, photos and private owner details and lets you choose where to save or send it (for example Google Drive or email). Keep that file safe, because it contains your private notes. We never receive it.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Deleting your data

Delete a listing inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted from where you saved them.

Children

The app is meant for adults who manage property and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
